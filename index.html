LiveSub

On-Device • No Cloud

Live subtitles for anything your phone plays.

Edge AI • Sherpa-ONNX • ML Kit • Jetpack Compose

A private, offline speech-to-subtitle translator for Android. It listens to a video, stream, game or call, turns speech into text, translates it, and shows the result in a floating subtitle window over any app. Speech recognition and translation both run on the phone: no account, no API key, no audio uploaded.

Two-Speed Speech Recognition Instant mode streams words as they are spoken. About 1 second mode uses a Silero voice detector to cut phrases and decodes each one whole with SenseVoice, Moonshine, Parakeet, GigaAM or Whisper, with punctuation.
Phone Audio Capture Records any app's audio via AudioPlaybackCapture (or the mic), resampled to 16 kHz with an anti-aliasing filter and a voice clean-up stage tuned for speech.
Sentence-Aware Translation Joins pause-split fragments into whole sentences, filters music and noise text, then translates through an ordered ML Kit queue with automatic language detection.
Floating Overlay Compose UI in a system overlay with Roll-up, Movie and Live subtitle styles, a Quick Settings tile, and on-phone history in Room.

Net One Tap

No Airplane Mode

Mobile internet stuck? Fix it in one tap.

Android • Kotlin • Shizuku / Root • Telephony

Restarts only the mobile radio, the same fix as toggling airplane mode, while Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and mobile data stay untouched. Also shows a per-SIM 5G status notification.

Privileged Radio Control Calls the hidden ITelephony.setRadioForSubscriber through Shizuku or root, with a soft-refresh fallback. Skips the reset during calls and always brings the radio back.
5G Status Detection Reads hidden NR state and frequency from ServiceState (the same values as the *#*#4636#*#* screen) with dual-SIM support and no location permission.

Offline Reel Captions

Offline • Indian Languages

Auto subtitles for Tamil, Tanglish and more, made on the phone.

Whisper • IndicTrans2 • ONNX Runtime • Media3

Pick a video and a language, and the app writes the captions: in the original script, in English letters by sound ("vanakkam nanbargale"), or translated. Fix and style every caption, then export an MP4 with the captions burned in, or an .srt file. Built for South Indian creators. The video never leaves the phone.

Speech Models per Language Whisper fine-tunes for Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Hindi and English via Sherpa-ONNX, plus a Tanglish model that keeps English words in English. Silero VAD cuts the audio at pauses.
English Letters by Sound A rule engine for each script writes the words as they sound, including changes the letters don't show (Tamil க is "k" in kaadhal but "g" in magan).
On-Phone Translation IndicTrans2 on ONNX Runtime translates sentence by sentence, then spreads each meaning over the captions by speaking time, so the English keeps pace with the talk.
Caption Editor & Export Karaoke-style word timing, motion, 16 fonts with matching Tamil looks, a full edit history, and MP4 export on the phone's video hardware (Media3 Transformer).

Artisan NPR Studio

No AI • No Internet

Turn any photo into a sketch, painting or comic.

Kotlin • Compose • OpenGL ES 3.0 • GLSL

134 art styles in 12 groups: pencil sketch, oil paint, watercolour, halftone, pixel art, film and glitch looks. Each style has its own controls, and a high-resolution export looks just like the preview. The APK is about 2 MB and asks for no permissions.

One Shader per Style Each style is a GPU fragment shader built on known image-art methods: XDoG ink, Kuwahara painting, line integral convolution brush strokes, CMYK halftone screens, dithering and Voronoi mosaics.
Fast on a Mid-Range Phone A 1280 px preview renders in 13 ms on average. Wide effects read from pre-blurred copies of the photo, so a 3200 px export matches the preview.

Live Wallpaper Maker

Light on Battery

Design your own live wallpaper: layers, tilt parallax and 3D scene transitions.

Kotlin • Compose • OpenGL ES 3.0 • MediaCodec

Stack videos, GIFs, photos, gradients, particles and a live clock, give each layer its own tilt and swipe depth, add looks and keyframe animation, and switch between scenes on a timer, on unlock, on double-tap or by time of day, with crossfade, 3D flip and cube transitions. Creations can be shared as a file.

Heavy Work Once, at Import Videos and GIFs are trimmed and baked into seamless-loop MP4s on the GPU and the phone's encoder (decoder → OpenGL → encoder surface), with the crossfade or ping-pong built in. HDR clips are tone-mapped and rotated phone videos come out upright.

SEA MOBA 5v5 Tournament

Web App • Live

Sign-ups, teams and brackets for a Mobile Legends tournament, in one phone-friendly web app.

React • TypeScript • Firebase • Gemini Vision

Players sign in with an email link, confirm their game ID, upload three stats screenshots and pay the entry fee by UPI. They form 5-player teams with a 6-digit code, then play through a seeded bracket, shown as a tree with colour-coded best-of rounds, and report each result with screenshots. On match day each team's page shows the next opponent and start time, players check in, and winners get confetti until their next match. Admins handle payments, reviews, refunds, brackets, disputes and emergency substitutes from one dashboard, and every admin change is logged.

Screenshots Read by Gemini Gemini 3 Flash on Vertex AI reads each stats screenshot right after upload. The app checks the numbers and the in-game name against the player's profile (even names written in decorative fonts) and either says how to fix it ("set the mode to Ranked") or flags it for an admin.
Fair Seeding Every player gets a 0–100 score from stars and stats compared with the whole field. Team power seeds single elimination, double elimination or balanced groups, so the strongest teams meet late.
Results & Disputes One captain reports the score with a screenshot per game; the other confirms or disputes. If nobody answers in time, a scheduled job accepts it, moves the winner on and crowns the champion. The same job raises admin alerts for disputes and teams that haven't checked in.
Rules on the Server Team, payment, result and admin changes go through Cloud Functions in Firestore transactions, behind Firestore and Storage security rules. 249 tests run against the Firebase emulators.
Up to 50 Teams Mobile-First Firebase + Vertex AI
